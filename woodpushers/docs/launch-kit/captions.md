## Story pack (day 1)

1. Screenshot of the map with pins + "this is every place to play in
   Paris" + link sticker.
2. Poll: "Chess in a bar or in a park?" (engagement primer)
3. Repost the carousel with "DM me your hidden chess spot, I'll add it".

## Posting rhythm for launch month

3-4 posts/week: alternate city spotlights (as new cities get enriched,
each becomes a carousel in 10 minutes from admin data), trip content, one
player portrait per week. Stories daily when traveling. Reply to every
comment with a question back.
