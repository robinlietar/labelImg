# ChessNow launch and growth plan

Positioning in one line: **find a real chess game anywhere, tonight.**
The product's magic moment is landing somewhere and having a board and an
opponent within the hour. Every piece of content should prove that moment.

## Assets already built into the product

- Share buttons everywhere (app, places, cities) with clean copy.
- City pages that work as landing pages (`/city/paris`) with map, venues,
  calendar and the WhatsApp join button: link these in every post.
- WhatsApp communities in Sydney and Paris (~120 players) as the seed
  audience and the distribution channel for every launch.
- Venue photos, ratings and hours from Google enrichment: content-ready
  visuals for city spotlight posts.

## Instagram (primary channel)

Handle: reserve `@chessnow.app` (fallbacks: `@getchessnow`,
`@chessnowapp`). Bio: "Find over-the-board chess anywhere. Map, players,
events." + link to the app.

Content pillars, in ratio:
1. **City spotlights (40%)**: carousel per city, "Where to play chess in
   Lisbon", 5 venues with photos, hours, one insider tip. End card: QR +
   city page link. The venue database IS the content pipeline.
2. **The chess trip (30%)**: travel diary format. Land, open the app, find a
   game, film the game and the person. "Game found in 43 minutes." This is
   the strongest possible product demo and it humanizes the app.
3. **Player stories (20%)**: 30-second portraits of regulars from the
   communities: the 1200 who plays every lunch break, the 2000 who hustles
   blitz in the park.
4. **Product moments (10%)**: new city launched, event calendar, open-now
   filter. Show the screen for 3 seconds max, always inside a story.

Formats: Reels over stills (reach), carousels for city guides (saves),
stories for community reposts. Post 3-4x/week during launch month.

## Launch sequence

1. **Week 0**: soft launch inside the Sydney and Paris WhatsApp groups.
   Ask every member to create a profile and heart their home venue. Goal:
   the two launch cities look alive before any stranger arrives.
2. **Week 1**: Instagram goes live with the two launch-city spotlight
   carousels + one reel filmed at a Paris venue (Blitz Society) and one at
   a Sydney venue. Cross-post to personal accounts.
3. **Week 2+**: the chess trip becomes the engine (below).
4. Each new city that reaches ~10 venues gets its spotlight post and its
   WhatsApp group created in admin.

## The chess trip as growth engine

Each city visited follows the same loop:
- Before arriving: check the app's venues, post a story "heading to X,
  where should I play?" (engagement + local intel).
- On site: play at 2-3 venues, film short clips, meet the local organizer,
  create the city's WhatsApp group WITH them (a local admin is what makes
  a group survive), leave QR cards at the venues.
- After: city spotlight carousel using the trip photos, tag the venues and
  players. The city page now has a real community attached.
- In-app: the trip itself demonstrates "visiting" mode (availability status
  is already built for exactly this).

## Other channels, in order of effort/return

- **Reddit**: r/chess and city subreddits answer "where to play in X?"
  weekly. Genuine answers linking the city page, never spammy.
- **Lichess/chess.com clubs**: the linked-account base already exists;
  post city pages in club forums of visited cities.
- **QR posters/cards** at venues: a chessboard-patterned card, "Players
  meet here. See who." Cheap, targeted, permanent.
- **Local federations and club newsletters**: they want visibility for
  their open nights; the events calendar gives them a free listing.
- **Product Hunt / HN**: only after 10+ cities look alive; one shot.

## Measurement (already in the admin dashboard)

Users, active this week, messages, likely encounters, signals, city chat
requests. The single north-star: **encounters** (chats with meetup
language). Everything above exists to move that number.
